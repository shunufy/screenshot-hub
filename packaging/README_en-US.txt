Screenshot Hub 0.2.0 (English)

1. Double-click ScreenshotHub.exe.
2. The app automatically searches standard game and screenshot locations.
3. It uses Steam, Epic Games, HoYoPlay, and Windows installation information to find games,
   then detects folders such as ScreenShot, Screenshots, and PhotoMode.
4. Use Add folder for personal folders that are not registered by a launcher.

Screenshot Hub does not perform an unrestricted scan of your PC or entire drives.
Screenshot Hub does not modify images while creating previews and does not move, rename, edit, delete, or upload them.
Settings are stored at %LOCALAPPDATA%\ScreenshotHub\settings.json.

Formats: PNG / JPEG / BMP / GIF / TIFF
System: Windows 10/11 x64

This executable is unsigned. Verify its source and SHA-256 against the release notes.
