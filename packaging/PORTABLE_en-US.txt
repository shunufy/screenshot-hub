About the portable edition

The portable.flag file makes Screenshot Hub store settings, catalogs, favorites,
tags, and the analysis cache in the Data folder beside ScreenshotHub.exe.

- Extract the complete ZIP to a writable folder before starting the app.
- Move the whole folder together if you relocate the app.
- Image files are not copied into Data and are never moved, renamed, edited, or deleted.
- Deleting portable.flag switches the app back to the standard %LOCALAPPDATA% location.
